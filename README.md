# Mood Checker Module 😊😿

## Overview
The Mood Checker module analyzes pet behavior to detect emotional states
such as stress, anxiety, happiness, or discomfort.

## Key Features
- Behavior-based mood detection
- AI analysis of activity patterns
- Early stress and anxiety identification
- Mood improvement suggestions

## Purpose
To help pet owners better understand their pets' emotional well-being
and take timely actions to improve their quality of life.

## Technologies (Planned)
- AI behavior analysis
- Pattern recognition
- User-input and sensor-based data

## Outcome
Enhances emotional care and strengthens the bond between pets and owners.
